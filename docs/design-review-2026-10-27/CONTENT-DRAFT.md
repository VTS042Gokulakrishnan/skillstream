### Draft article: Prepare for your next video interview
A little preparation helps you focus on the conversation.

Check your setup.
Choose a quiet place and test your camera, microphone and connection. Close unrelated applications and make sure you understand how to join the session.

Prepare clear examples.
Review the role and choose examples from your experience. Describe the situation, your responsibility, the action you took and the result. Keep the examples accurate and relevant.

Practice out loud.
Speak through a few answers before the interview. Aim for clear explanations rather than memorized scripts. If you use a practice tool, review its recording and processing notice before starting.

Review and improve.
After practice, identify one or two things to improve. AI feedback can be useful but may be incomplete or incorrect; use it as one input to your own review.

CTA: Explore interview practice.
This is new proposed content, not an assertion that an existing article has been reviewed or published.

## Privacy Policy editorial draft
Title: Privacy Policy.
Introduction: This policy explains how [LEGAL OPERATOR] handles personal information when you use SkillStream.
Effective date: [APPROVED EFFECTIVE DATE].
Scope: [CONFIRMED MARKETS AND PRODUCTS].

Sections to complete:
1. Information provided by candidates: account and contact details, resume and profile data, applications and messages.
2. Information provided by employers: business and account details, job postings, recruitment activity and billing-related information.
3. Practice and assessment information: recordings, transcripts, code submissions, results and generated feedback. Specify exactly what each feature collects.
4. Purposes: operating accounts and features, processing purchases, supporting users, maintaining services and lawful obligations. Confirm applicable legal bases for relevant markets.
5. Recipients and visibility: explain employers, other users and providers by feature. State who sees practice separately from employer assessments.
6. AI services: identify actual providers, data sent, purpose, applicable retention controls and user choices. Do not call an API integration consumer ChatGPT indiscriminately.
7. Retention: specific periods or meaningful criteria for each data category, deletion, backup retention and legal holds.
8. Cookies and external services: essential storage, analytics if used, social login, embedded maps and outbound partner services.
9. Rights and requests: accessible process, identity verification, applicable rights, contact and appeal process where relevant.
10. International transfers: actual storage/processing locations and relevant safeguards if applicable.
11. Eligibility/children: align with approved minimum account age and actual onboarding.
12. Changes and contact: operator and monitored privacy channel.

No blanket GDPR/HIPAA/PIPEDA-compliant heading, invented retention periods or absolute no-storage/no-sharing claims.
